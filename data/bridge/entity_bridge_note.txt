Bridge note for hybrid GraphRAG demo (unstructured text).

This note repeats canonical entity names that also appear in the structured
CSV tables and in the PDF corpus under docs/, so exact-name resolution can
merge the two halves of the graph.

Carbon farming survey practices:
- Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) is a rice water-management practice that
  alternately wets and dries fields, reducing continuous flooding and methane.
- System of Rice Intensification (SRI) changes plant spacing, water, and soil
  management in rice systems and is discussed as a methane- and water-saving
  approach.
- Green Cane Trash Blanketing (GCTB) keeps sugarcane residue on the field as a
  trash blanket instead of burning it, supporting soil organic carbon.
- Carbon Farming is the umbrella set of agricultural best practices for
  sequestration and emission reduction. MRV means Monitoring, Reporting, and
  Verification.

Trade liberalization paper entities:
- China WTO accession is the policy event used as the trade-liberalization
  shock. The study estimates iceberg trade cost reductions and CES preference
  parameters for domestic and export markets, and discusses processing-trade
  robustness and firm exit probability in the empirical sample.

Electricity and carbon market-design paper entities:
- Austria 2025 baseline carbon-electricity expenditure reduction and
  Germany 2025 baseline carbon-electricity expenditure reduction are country
  baselines in the simulation year 2025.
- Hard price threshold activation rule creates bunching incentives around the
  activation point.
- Linear phase-in ramp of the deduction mitigates threshold bunching.
- Proposed settlement deduction rule for carbon-cost pass-through is the
  settlement mechanism intended to reduce consumer exposure to carbon-cost
  pass-through.
